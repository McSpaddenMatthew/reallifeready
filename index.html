Who it helps

For capable kids who need practical systems and ownership.

This is not tutoring, therapy, or a replacement for teachers or counselors. It is structured executive function coaching for daily follow-through at home and school.

Starting feels hard

Your child understands the work but has trouble beginning without repeated reminders.

Organization slips

Assignments, backpacks, planners, and materials become a constant source of friction.

Screens take over

Transitions away from devices are difficult, especially when homework or routines need attention.

Coaching

Fewer reminders. More responsibility. Less nightly friction.

Real Life Ready provides structured executive function coaching with practical parent support, routines, organization systems, screen-transition plans, and a follow-through scorecard.

Student coaching

Parent support

Parents get language, routines, and boundaries that reduce rescuing and repeating at home.

School/home systems

The work connects backpacks, planners, digital assignments, screens, routines, and responsibility in real life.

Questions parents ask

Clear boundaries, practical support.

Is this therapy?

No. Real Life Ready provides executive function coaching and practical skill-building. It does not diagnose or treat mental health conditions.

Is this tutoring?

No. The focus is not reteaching academic content. The focus is starting, planning, organizing, managing screens, and following through.

Do you work with teachers or counselors?

The work is designed to complement, not replace, school supports and counseling relationships.
